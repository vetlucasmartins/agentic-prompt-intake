# Example: Under-Specified Prompt

## Input

```text
Make something good for my brand with this.
```

## Expected response

```markdown
There is not enough information to execute without guessing too much.

Provisional brief:
- Objective: create something for the brand.
- Deliverable: undefined.
- Context: not provided.
- Audience: not provided.
- Constraints: not provided.
- Success criteria: not provided.

Essential questions:
1. What do you want to produce: copy, a campaign, identity, strategy, a post, a script, or a page?
2. Who is the brand trying to reach?
3. What source material should I use?
```
